## Day 1 — tonight (19 October)

> In our test case, three chronic medicines cost ₹1,850 a month. The same molecules at Jan Aushadhi prices: ₹620.
>
> We're building **MediSave** — an ASI:One agent that turns a prescription into a savings sheet: the molecule, the generic/Jan Aushadhi price, the nearest Kendra, and the questions to ask your doctor.

>
> Why an agent: it has to parse the prescription, map brands to molecules, compute the monthly saving, find the Kendra, and produce a document — in one chat.
